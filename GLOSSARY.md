# Session Picker

## Language

**Session**:
A conversation that can be resumed in its working directory.
_Avoid_: Transcript when referring to the conversation itself

**Rollout**:
Codex's saved record of a session, including its prompts, responses, and context.

**Human prompt**:
A user's request or reply in a session, distinct from automatically supplied context.

**Injected context**:
Instructions or environment information supplied automatically rather than requested by the user.

**Fork**:
A distinct session that continues from another session's history.

**Subagent session**:
A conversation delegated to an agent by another session, distinct from a directly opened user session.
